01 · Moisture swelling

Water enters and laminate swells.

Five inputs govern how much water is absorbed at a given time and how much in-plane swelling it produces.

02 · Thermal mismatch

Two materials have different expansion.

Temperature changes the free expansion of both materials. Their expansion difference creates the thermal mismatch strain.

03 · From strain to stress

The two pathways come together.

Moisture swelling and thermal mismatch combine. Two stiffness inputs scale the resulting stress index.
